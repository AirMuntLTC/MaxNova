MaxNova Widget

Get quick access to chat, media, and voice with MaxNova.

What it does: When enabled in the native Android app, the MaxNova home-screen widget gives you three instant actions.

  • Chat: opens MaxNova, starts a new conversation, and focuses the keyboard.
  • Media: opens MaxNova, starts a new conversation, and opens the media picker.
  • Voice: opens MaxNova, starts a new conversation, and starts microphone input.

Android may ask you to confirm adding the widget to your home screen. The widget is a native Android feature; the browser version cannot place a system widget by itself.

Kids Mode

MaxNova Kids Mode

Turns MaxNova into a friendly, age-appropriate learning assistant.

Enable Kids ModeOff

What Kids Mode does

When enabled, MaxNova is instructed to act like a patient kids teacher. Answers are simplified, encouraging, educational, and age-appropriate.

  • Focuses on school subjects, learning, creativity, stories, science, math, language, general knowledge, and safe everyday questions.
  • Avoids adult sexual content, explicit adult themes, graphic violence, instructions for dangerous activities, drugs, gambling, and other age-inappropriate material.
  • When a request is not appropriate for Kids Mode, MaxNova should refuse briefly and redirect the child toward a safe learning topic.
  • Kids Mode is an AI safety setting, not a replacement for active parental supervision, device controls, or age verification.

Parent / guardian guidance

Parents and guardians should supervise use, talk with children about sharing personal information, and use the device's own parental controls where appropriate. Do not allow children to share passwords, addresses, phone numbers, school credentials, payment information, or private family details with an AI.

Kids Mode is saved on this device until you switch it off. It does not make MaxNova perfect or guarantee that every answer is suitable, so supervision remains important.
